# Kolkhoz acts in the 1930s

Historical check for boss, 11 October 2026, task #586. **The proposed three-way hierarchy is not established as a historical rule.** Work assignments are well attested; the chairman versus specialist terminology, a universal order-book form and an obligatory first order are not. These findings do not change [the game's accepted orders design](../../../manual/design/chairman/orders.md) automatically.

## Chairman and board

The statute's §§19–22 distinguish elected collective bodies from the chairman's daily management. Section 17 refers to «постановлениям общего собрания и правления». Crucially, §12 also says «по приказу правления артели» for a bank withdrawal requiring chairman and bookkeeper signatures. **The claim that only the chairman could issue something called an order is therefore too broad.** These are specific powers, not a universal taxonomy of documents. See [the contemporary printed statute, pages 3–4](https://niklib.ru/wp-content/uploads/storage/pressa/kommunar/1935/21.pdf); [searchable transcription, §§12, 17, 19–24](https://rags.ru/documents/dop_documents/64/instr_27286/0/prime.html).

The handbook's accounting chapter says grain is dispatched under a «письменного распоряжения председателя колхоза». It also describes board and brigadier instructions. Thus «распоряжение» was not reserved to specialists. **This is 1941 evidence**, not a chairman's order preserved from 1931: [accounting chapter](https://istmat.org/node/25214), identified as 1941 in [the library's catalogue](https://istmat.org/node/21349).

## Work assignments and the paper form

The 1931 publication describes an assignment specifying brigade membership, work type, location and deadline; the brigadier receives it, allocates responsibility, supervises, evaluates and records completed work. It says «Бригадир доводит наряд до каждого колхозника путем личного инструктирования перед началом работ». This establishes who assigns work, not who physically pens every document. [Kolhoztsentr provisions published 8 February 1931](https://istmat.org/node/37212), reproduced from *Советская юстиция*, 1931, No. 5, p. 24; the editor specifically identifies the date as publication, not necessarily adoption.

The 1935 statute confirms the brigadier's allocation and weekly labor-day records; §24 assigns accounting to the bookkeeper. It does not establish a separate tally clerk as dispatcher. The game's clerk combines functions deliberately; **do not restore brigade leaders to the game to match the source**. This is an adaptation boundary, not a proposed mechanic.

A useful specimen is P. Lepilov's article on spring work in Mineralovodsky district, *На фронте коллективизации*, No. 9, form on printed p. 46 (PDF page 48). The agronomist prepares a brigade assignment; its table lists work, area, completion period and daily people, draught animals, ploughs, harrows and seeders. It is a **reported local example**, not a national compulsory blank. [Readable scan](https://istmat.org/files/uploads/25934/no_9.pdf); [repository copy labelled 1930](https://history.org.ua/LiberUA/NaFrKol9_1930/NaFrKol9_1930.pdf). The article/form text was readable, but the issue's cover date was not verified; retain that bibliographical limit. It cannot supply a confirmed dated chairman's order.

## Newly elected chairman and the demo

Election belongs to the general meeting under §20; the statute does not prescribe the requested self-issued taking-office formula. The game's «Приказ № 1» should therefore be presented as **authored period-style content**, not a quotation or mandatory historical ceremony.

Recommendation to boss: keep the human-approved game labels if desired, but remove claims that this exact three-tier classification and first-order formula are historically proved. For the demo, use a modest header, number, two clear clauses and chairman's signature; these are proposed presentation choices, not an authenticated template. The April 1931 month/year/weekday date is required by the game's calendar, not by real 1930s record-keeping. The clerk's appointment by the player is the accepted game decision; historical brigadier practice does not override it.

## Limits of the check

Search covered the statute, 1931 work-organisation provisions, accounting forms/manual leads, and archival order-book/first-order queries. Mixed-date fonds and later chairman instructions were not treated as 1930s specimens. The 1935 edition's existence/date is also recorded by [the Russian State Library](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01005299861), but a catalogue entry alone does not establish a document's text. The 1934 accounting-forms appendix was located but could not be read fully: [20 November 1934, No. 2599](https://www.istmat.org/node/43074). It supports no findings here. **Not found means unconfirmed in this check, not proof that the practice never existed.** No historical punitive or political passages are proposed for the game's text.
