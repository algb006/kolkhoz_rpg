EVIDENCE AND ITS LIMITS

S1 — official archival inventory, read in full through the web PDF extraction:
https://gosarhivrme.ru/sites/default/files/opisi/R247_op1.pdf
State Archive of Mari El, fond R-247, inventory 1, agricultural artel, records 1930–1933. Inventory page 2 lists file 9, correspondence concerning members' spring seed contributions (1930–1931). Page 3: file 20, spring-sowing summaries (1931); file 21, residents' applications to the board (1931–1932); file 29, village-council circulars (1932–1933). Page 4: files 33/45, applications to the board/for joining (1932/1933). These prove catalogued document categories, not the literal headers, registration stamps or mandatory forms of the underlying files. They are local evidence, not a nationwide instruction.

S2 — primary 1930 circular, searchable transcription read, including archival provenance:
https://istmat.org/node/31815
23 October 1930, reproduced from a documentary edition, volume 2, p. 692; original cited as RGAE fond 7486, inventory 1, file 9, leaves 73–73 ob. It addresses district land departments and kolkhoz unions and explicitly discusses applications to join an artel, board consideration and general-meeting confirmation. This establishes циркуляр and заявление in the relevant administrative context. It does not establish a uniform incoming register at every individual kolkhoz. Its historical deadline and governing bodies do not override the game's accepted calendar or resident-request rules.

S3 — period telephonogram catalogue lead, metadata/index excerpt only; body unavailable:
https://docs.historyrussia.org/ru/nodes/441751
The historical-document library labels a communication dated not earlier than 16 October 1931 as Телефонограмма. This is evidence for a period document category in higher-level administration, NOT proof of a standard village telephone-message blank or that a phone call automatically became a written telephonogram. Retain the game's term conditionally for a recorded message conveyed by telephone; ordinary speech over the telephone is not itself a document. This last distinction is a semantic recommendation, not an authenticated 1931 village instruction.

S4 — reply-number specimen lead, search-index text only; full body unavailable:
https://istmat.org/node/61457
A letter dated 25 February 1931 has the indexed heading fragment «На Ваш № 75/с-13/II—31 г.». It is not a kolkhoz letter. It supports a modest phrase lead, not a universal mandatory reply rule. The report's objection to «Ваш вх.» is chiefly a reference-ownership inference: the number recorded as incoming by the receiver is not automatically the sender's own number. No historical template or additional game binding is claimed from this fragment.

S5 — bibliographical lead for reports, catalogue/index only; book not read:
https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01009019401
The Russian State Library identifies a Kolhoztsentr instruction for compiling the kolkhoz annual report for 1930, published 1930. It confirms the existence/topic of that edition, not its specific columns, submission dates, signatories or reference numbering.

WORD VERDICTS

Письмо / переписка: supported as a document category by S1; no evidence requires replacing the demo's district plan letter with циркуляр or разнарядка. A план is the subject/content, not a necessary name for the physical covering document. The exact fictional first letter is authored game content.
Заявление: supported by S1/S2 for board applications and joining. Do not extrapolate a mandatory separate chairman-addressed application for every wedding, house or vacation; those particular game requests follow their own accepted design, not this historical check.
Сводка: supported locally by S1 for sowing progress. Отчёт has a contemporary publication lead in S5; the game's routine-report standing order is not historically proved by it.
Циркуляр: supported by S1/S2. Suitable for a circulated rule/instruction, not a compulsory replacement for every district letter.
Телефонограмма: period catalogue attestation S3; local form/recording procedure unverified. Use after the game's telephone unlock without adding a new decision or claiming every phone conversation is recorded.
Заявка / извещение / справка / жалоба / телеграмма: no authenticated local1930s specimen or universal form established in this bounded check. These are understandable existing game labels; unconfirmed here is not a finding that the words did not exist. No unilateral renaming is proposed.
Входящие / исходящие, Вх./Исх. №: exact local1931 register specimen and abbreviations not authenticated here. Annual numbering from January, two tabs, year folder, automatic entry and waiting/answered/filed statuses are the human-approved presentation. Keep them, but do not label them a proved universal historical blank.
