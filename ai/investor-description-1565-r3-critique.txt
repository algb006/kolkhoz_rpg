Investor description #1565 — R3 editorial critique
2026-11-05. Written before the replacement text.

The R2 pitch explains its own intentions instead of showing the game. Its
opening promises a village; the following pages replace that village with
abstract responsibility, consequences and repeated reassurance about scope.
The camera gets more room than leisure, sport and population change combined.
The differentiator (a chairman with an origin, skills and consequential roles)
arrives too late and is then repeated in the positioning section.

Cut: repeated promises of warmth and immersion; calendar placeholders; the
long genre comparison; qualifications at the start of every late-game
paragraph; repetitions of responsibility and delegation; procedural detail
about the Road Home nodes that does not help the reader want the game.
Retain at most the arrival image. Rebuild every other section from its subject.

Add: the region as usable land at believable distances; the actual journey of
a load; the connection between road, labour and construction; household life;
births, schooling, marriages, ageing, arrivals and departure requests; summer
swimming, winter leisure, club, bathhouse, dance, sport and holidays. Give
these subjects decisions and visible outcomes, not a facilities inventory.

Put the RPG promise near the opening. Give all eight origins distinct human
starting points; show how skills and delegation change the chairman's work.
Keep ideological, humanist and shadow paths concrete and distinct. State the
goal of a long, absorbing, replayable game explicitly. Retain voluntary
endings, Road Home, the sequel vision and the phased roadmap at the end.

Authority: the human's R3 correction defines epochs by way of life. 1931 is
the start; 1940s–50s and 1960s–80s describe visual reference, not transition
dates. The camera correction from R2 remains binding.

Canon guardrails: do not turn brief examples into invented accepted quests.
Use existing wedding, sport, electrification and village-life material;
identify illustrative scene proposals together, if needed. Disease is known
through conversation, never shown as a scene of suffering. Real distances
do not imply real-time ageing: the life cycle is deliberately compressed.
Do not promise unrestricted construction over river, protected sites or
unusable terrain. Mark the late-era framework and sequel vision once each.

Hostile reread criteria: every paragraph supplies a new picture, action or
product fact; no duplicate sales conclusion; no unsupported implementation
claim; no economy figures, development dates or durations; roadmap last.
